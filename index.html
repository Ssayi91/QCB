01 // The Science of Precision

Understanding the Impact of Exact Measurements.

In industrial and laboratory settings, a fraction of a millimeter or a single degree of temperature can dictate product safety, regulatory compliance, and operational efficiency. We bridge the gap between theoretical standards and practical application.

Calibration & Quality Assurance

Accredited by the Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS), we ensure your instruments meet the strictest international tolerances. Our methodology guarantees that every reading is legally and scientifically defensible.

  • Mass & Weighing Scales
  • Pressure & Temperature Gauges
  • Centrifuges & pH Meters
  • Tesla Meters (Heavy Metals)

Repair, Maintenance & Auditing

Beyond calibration, we provide comprehensive lifecycle management for your critical equipment. Our energy and air auditing services utilize advanced Carbon Monoxide and humidity meters to ensure workplace safety and industrial conservation.

  • Analog / Digital Weighing Repair
  • Ovens, Incubators & Autoclaves
  • Air Quality & Noise Level Mapping
  • Remedial Conservation Measures

Backed by Regional & International Standards

Our technical personnel hold advanced certifications, including specialized autoclave and sterilization training from NLA South Africa, ensuring your operations meet global benchmarks.
